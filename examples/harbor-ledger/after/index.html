How it works

The book behind your counter, minus the carbon paper.

  1. 01

    Write it down

    When a regular takes something on credit, add the amount to their page. It takes a few seconds between customers.

  2. 02

    See who owes what

    Every customer’s balance and how long it has been open, in one list. Overdue accounts are marked.

  3. 03

    Remind, politely

    Send a payment reminder by WhatsApp with the exact amount, so you don’t have to bring it up at the counter.
