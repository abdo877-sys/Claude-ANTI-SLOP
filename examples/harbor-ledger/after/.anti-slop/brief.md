# Creative brief — Harbor Ledger (fix-ai-generated example)

_Product facts come from benchmark scenario 01's description. Everything else
is marked (assumed)._

## Evidence
- Product: a bookkeeping app for small shops in Djibouti; owners track the
  credit they give to regular customers and send payment reminders by WhatsApp.
- Core object on screen: **a page of the shop's credit book** — customer, what
  they owe, since when.
- Audience & context: shop owners (épiceries, boutiques), on a phone, between
  customers (assumed). Trust is personal; credit is a relationship, not a loan.
- Business objective: get shop owners to request access.
- Primary action: request access (contact page — the only real route).
- Locales: French and Arabic (product), seed page is English → kept English here;
  FR/AR versions are listed as follow-up work.
- Existing brand assets: none besides the name.
- Real content available: product description only. Missing: pricing,
  user numbers, testimonials, screenshots, legal pages.

## DNA
Harbor Ledger helps small shop owners in Djibouti keep track of who owes them
what, so they can be paid without awkward conversations. They arrive busy,
on a phone, used to a paper credit book. They trust things that look like the
tools they already use.

## Tensions
1. precise ↔ warm — **60% precise**: it's money, amounts must be exact; but it's neighbours, not debtors.
2. crafted ↔ technical — **70% crafted**: the reference is the paper book behind the counter, not fintech.
3. quiet ↔ loud — **70% quiet**: reminding people about money must feel polite.

## Direction
- Family: **Swiss International** (clarity of amounts and names) + one borrowed
  move from **Organic** (real paper material instead of screens).
- Evidence: (1) the core task is reading names and amounts quickly → Swiss clarity;
  (2) users' current tool is a paper credit book → material metaphor; (3) trust is personal → no corporate fintech sheen.
- Material metaphor: **the duplicate receipt book** (carnet à souches) — off-white
  original, yellow carbon copy, blue carbon ink, rubber-stamp red.
- Signature move: **the carbon duplicate** — key figures and numbers carry a
  faint, slightly offset blue copy, like carbon paper. Used on: the specimen
  total, the step numbers, the closing headline amount. Never on body text or buttons.

## System decisions
- Type: voice IBM Plex Sans Condensed 600 (printed form headings) · reading IBM
  Plex Sans 400/600 · data IBM Plex Mono 500 for amounts — one family keeps it
  Swiss; condensed + mono echo printed forms. Has an Arabic sibling (IBM Plex
  Sans Arabic) for the AR version.
- Palette (sampled from the receipt book): paper #F4F1E8, copy-yellow #F2E6B4,
  carbon ink #1D2433, carbon blue #2C3E8F (accent), stamp red #A8322A (overdue only),
  rule #CFC7B2.
- Shape: square containers, 2px on controls — paper has corners.
- Depth: flat + hairline rules + one tone step (the yellow copy). No shadows.
- Motion: paper settles (200ms ease-out); the overdue stamp lands once (120ms). Reduced motion: none.
- Layout: 7/5 statement + specimen hero; numbered ruled sequence; ledger rows; plain closing.

## Refusals
- No gradients, blobs, glass, glow, emoji, sparkles.
- No invented proof: no user counts, uptime, logos, testimonials, pricing.
- No dark theme for this page (the brand is paper).
- No feature cards; nothing that isn't in the product description.
- No stock photos of people with laptops.

## Checks
- Default-look axes matched: 0/10 (accent, background, gradient, type, shape,
  depth, layout, icons, motion, copy all differ).
- Sibling test: run on scenario 07 (solar mini-grid dashboard) the same process
  yields rack-label/LED industrial direction on dark surfaces — unrelated.
